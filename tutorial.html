How to use Node Labs

Follow these simple steps to map out your A/V setup.

1. Adding Nodes

Click a device from the left sidebar to add it to your canvas. It will spawn exactly in the center of your screen.

2. Connecting Cables

Click and drag from one node's output port to another node's input port to route a signal.

3. Navigation & Touch

Use two fingers to pinch and zoom smoothly. Drag with one finger (or hold spacebar with a mouse) to pan around the canvas.
