How to use Node Labs

Follow these simple steps to map out your A/V setup efficiently.

1. Adding Nodes

Click a device from the left sidebar to add it to your canvas. It will now spawn exactly in the center of your screen, no matter how far you have panned or zoomed.

2. Connecting Cables

Click and drag from one node's output port to another node's input port to route a signal. The bezier curve will automatically draw and show flow direction.

3. Navigation & Touch Controls

  • Desktop: Click and drag the background to pan. Use your mouse wheel to zoom in and out.
  • Touchscreens: Use one finger to pan around the canvas. Use two fingers to pinch and zoom smoothly around the center of your screen.

4. Viewer Mode

If you share your diagram with the ?viewer=true URL parameter, the sidebar will hide, and users will only be able to pan and zoom. They cannot move nodes or delete cables.
